Questions

What founders and investors ask before they trust a Skip list.

Where does the data come from?

From Cala's graph of companies, funding rounds and investors. Each fact keeps the page Cala found it on, ranked from primary source to aggregator.

What counts as a conflict?

A fund backed one of your direct competitors, or one of its people sits on that competitor's board.

Does the AI make up investors or numbers?

No. Gemini only judges which candidates are direct competitors and writes briefs from facts already fetched. Scores are plain code.

What if Cala is missing something?

The report says so: "Cala doesn't say who led", "Round sizes unknown". Early-stage Nordic coverage varies, so check a fund you know first.

What does it cost?

Nothing while it runs as our Junction 2026 build. There are no accounts.
